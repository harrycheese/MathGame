Delete Your Six-7 Account

You can permanently delete your Six-7 account and all associated personal data directly in the app at any time.

How to Delete Your Account Immediately

  1. Open Six-7 (on Android or the web) and sign in to your account.
  2. Tap Profile & Stats on the home screen.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete Account.
  4. Re-authenticate (enter your password or confirm with Google) and type DELETE to confirm.

What Data Is Deleted

  • Your Firebase Authentication account (email, display name, and sign-in credentials).
  • Your Cloud Firestore profile, career statistics, and all saved game session records.
  • Local progress data cached on your device.

What Data May Be Retained

Aggregated, anonymized analytics metrics previously collected via Google Analytics for Firebase are not linked to your deleted account and cannot be used to identify you.
