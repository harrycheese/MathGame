This Privacy Policy describes how Six-7 ("we", "our", or "the app") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the Six-7 web application or Android app.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: When you sign up using Email/Password or Google Sign-In via Firebase Authentication, we store your account identifier (UID), email address, and display name.
  • Gameplay & Progress Data: We store your scores, level progress, career statistics, operator mastery, and game session history in Google Cloud Firestore and locally on your device for offline access.
  • Usage Analytics: We use Google Analytics for Firebase to collect anonymized app usage and gameplay events (such as game mode, score, and session outcomes) to improve game balance and performance.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Authenticate your account and sync your gameplay progress across sessions.
  • Display your personal career statistics inside the app.
  • Analyze aggregate gameplay trends and fix bugs or performance issues.

3. Data Sharing & Third-Party Services

We do not sell your personal information. Your data is processed by Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Hosting, and Analytics) solely to operate the app, subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Data Retention & Account Deletion

We retain your account and gameplay data while your account is active. You can permanently delete your account and all associated Firestore progress and session data at any time inside the app under Profile → Delete Account, or by following the instructions on our Account Deletion page.
